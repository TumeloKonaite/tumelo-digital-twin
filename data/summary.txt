## Featured Projects

### LetsGo South Africa - AI-Powered Tourism Platform

Website: https://letsgodb.web.app/

I built a full-stack tourism web application for LetsGo South Africa, enabling admins to create, manage, and publish travel packages while customers browse packages, submit online enquiries, and chat with an AI agent for travel-related questions.

The platform includes a FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL database, React/Vite frontend, authentication, image handling, enquiry workflows, and cloud deployment. The goal is to give the tourism business a modern platform for package discovery, admin content management, customer enquiries, and AI-assisted travel support.

### BeautyVerse - Beauty Services Marketplace

Website: https://beautyverse.co.za/

BeautyVerse is a beauty services marketplace project where providers can create and manage service listings, while customers can browse beauty services and submit enquiries. The project demonstrates my ability to design marketplace-style platforms with reusable backend architecture, listing management, categories, authentication, image handling, and customer enquiry flows.

The system was built around a FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL database, SQLAlchemy/Alembic models and migrations, Docker-based local development, role-based authentication concepts, and a frontend for public marketplace discovery and provider-side listing management.

### MedDesk - AI Clinical Intake Proof of Concept

Website: https://meddesk.co.za/

I built a proof-of-concept clinical intake system where patients can chat with an AI assistant to report symptoms before seeing a practitioner. The system collects Subjective and patient-reported Objective information, stores the conversation, detects red-flag symptoms, and prepares structured draft SOAP notes for clinician review.

The project is designed as clinician-support software, not as a replacement for medical judgment. Its purpose is to structure intake information, surface red flags, and help clinicians review patient-reported information more efficiently.
